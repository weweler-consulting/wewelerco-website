02
General

This privacy policy applies to the website weweler.co and all its subpages. We process personal data only to the extent necessary for a functioning website, for answering your enquiries and for our services.

The legal bases are the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Spanish data protection act (Ley Orgánica 3/2018 de Protección de Datos Personales y garantía de los derechos digitales, LOPDGDD).

The website does not load content from third-party servers unless you open the appointment booking yourself (section 5). The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics tools. The only exception: the “Vivienda Radazul” page stores your language choice in your browser’s local storage (entry “lang”) so it is kept for your next visit. This information stays in your browser and is not transmitted to us.

03
Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted on our own server at Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. When a page is requested, the server automatically processes the following data:

  • IP address
  • date and time of the request
  • requested page and amount of data transferred
  • referrer URL (the previously visited page)
  • browser type, browser version and operating system

We need this data to deliver the website and keep it secure. We do not combine it with other data. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and stable operation of the website). The log files are deleted as soon as they are no longer needed for these purposes.

A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Hetzner.

04
Contact by email, phone or messenger

If you contact us by email, phone or messenger, we process your details (for example name, contact details, content of the message) to answer your enquiry and any follow-up questions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry relates to a contract or its preparation, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering your enquiry).

Some subpages contain links to WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Limited). Data is only transmitted to WhatsApp once you click such a link. WhatsApp’s privacy policy applies there.

05
Appointment booking via Google Calendar

To book a call, we use the appointment booking page of Google Calendar. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”).

The booking page is only loaded on our website once you click a booking button. Before that, no data is transmitted to Google. From the click onwards, Google receives technical data such as your IP address and browser information. Google may set cookies in the process. If you book an appointment, Google and we process the details you enter in the form (name, email address and any further details) as well as the chosen time slot. You receive a confirmation by email with a calendar invitation.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures taken at your request). We base the loading of the booking page on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in simple appointment scheduling). You trigger it yourself with your click.

Google may also transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). We use Google Calendar as part of Google Workspace, for which Google’s data processing terms apply.

The call itself takes place by video via Google Meet, also as part of Google Workspace. Google processes image and audio data, your name, your email address and technical connection data. We do not record calls without your explicit consent.

Without Google, you can arrange an appointment at any time by email to justus@weweler.co. More information: policies.google.com/privacy.

No automated decision-making, including profiling, pursuant to Art. 22 GDPR takes place.
