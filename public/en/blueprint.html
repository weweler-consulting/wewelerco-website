  • Every entry has a source.

    Source, date and status are recorded. Nothing is silently overwritten.

  • Client data stays where it belongs.

    A correction is recorded as a rule. New methods apply only after review and sign-off.

07

Approvals

Management keeps the decision. Assignment, draft, review, approval and execution are separate steps.

1 · AssignmentManagement or team lead
2 · DraftSpecialist role
3 · ReviewReview role
4 · ApprovalManagement
5 · ExecutionAuthorised person or tested workflow
6 · Feedbackinto the memory

Start at level 1

Drafts only. Nothing is sent, published or changed on its own.

More room is earned.

After ten results in a row without correction, the team lead proposes a higher level. Management decides, never automatically.

Approval is not execution.

An approved text is not yet the right to send it. Rights are limited technically, not just by instruction.
